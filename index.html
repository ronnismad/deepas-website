01 — About · আমাদের কথা

Two sisters, one mother's dream

দীপা'স K · M
Kamelia & Mistu Est. with love
মায়ের স্বপ্ন

Deepa's is an initiative of two sisters, Kamelia and Mistu. The name is their late mother's first name — Deepa — whose dream was to open a boutique of her own.

For years they bought boutique sarees that looked lovely in the shop and frayed after one wash, at prices that never quite matched what they were getting. Unsatisfied with that, the sisters decided to start their own boutique: one built to make genuinely high-quality sarees affordable, and reachable by everyone.

So they went to the looms themselves. Every Deepa's saree is traditionally handwoven by artisan families across Bengal's great weaving hubs — GI-tagged Shantipur, Begampur and Dhaniakhali cotton, the narrative Baluchari silk of Bishnupur, Murshidabad silk, and hand-stitched kantha from Santiniketan. No powerloom shortcuts, no middlemen.

Maa's name is on the door. Her dream is in every drape.

02 — The loom road · তাঁতের পথ

Six towns, one handloom belt

Our sarees are woven traditionally in the great saree-weaving hubs of West Bengal — in family looms that have been running for generations, most of them GI-tagged for exactly the cloth they make.

  1. 01

    শান্তিপুর Shantipur

    Nadia district · cotton tant

    Weaving here dates to 1409. The Shantipuri sari has carried a GI tag since 2009 and is still folded the old way, in the Guti Bhanj.

    GI tagged
  2. 02

    ফুলিয়া Phulia

    Nadia district · Tangail & jamdani

    The other half of the Shantipur cluster: 12,000 looms and 36,000 weavers, the densest stretch of handlooms in Bengal.

    Shantipur cluster
  3. 03

    বেগমপুর Begampur

    Hooghly district · cotton tant

    Matapar and Booty borders on a khatkhti loom, a craft recorded here since the 16th century and GI-tagged in 2026.

    GI tagged
  4. 04

    ধনিয়াখালি Dhaniakhali

    Hooghly district · cotton tant

    100 × 100 thread count, a 1.5–2 inch border and the traditional grey body — the plain, honest everyday saree.

    GI tagged
  5. 05

    বিষ্ণুপুর Bishnupur

    Bankura district · Baluchari silk

    Terracotta temple scenes lifted straight onto the pallu — Mahabharata episodes woven into silk over five or six days. GI since 2011.

    GI tagged
  6. 06

    মুর্শিদাবাদ Murshidabad

    Murshidabad district · mulberry silk

    Bengal's silk town since the 13th century and the source of Garad, Korial and Baluchari — fine, light and easy to drape.

    Silk country

05 — Terms · শর্তাবলী

Small shop, plain rules

Ordering & payment

Orders are confirmed once payment is received. We accept UPI, cards and bank transfer. Because every saree is handwoven, pieces are reserved for 48 hours while you decide.

Shipping within India

Dispatched from Kolkata within 2–4 working days and delivered across India in roughly a week. Handloom pieces travel folded in muslin, never in plastic.

Returns & exchange

Unworn sarees with tags intact may be returned within 7 days of delivery for an exchange or credit. Sale and altered pieces are final.

Caring for handloom cloth

Soak cotton tant once in cold salted water to set the colour, wash mild, starch lightly and dry in shade. Dry-clean silks. Slubs, uneven selvedge and slight shade variation are the signature of the hand, not a defect.

Authenticity

Where a cloth carries a Geographical Indication, we name the town it comes from and the tag it holds. If a piece turns out not to be handwoven as described, we will refund it in full.
